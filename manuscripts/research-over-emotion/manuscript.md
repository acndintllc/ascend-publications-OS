:::chapter-opener eyebrow="Volume I"
# Research Over Emotion
:::

When a finding contradicts a feeling, the feeling is the variable, not the
finding. This is the first habit of the discipline [@ascend2025rs1].

:::pullquote cite="Field log, 2025"
Evidence does not arrive on a schedule that flatters us.
:::

:::section title="The Method"

We hold the question open longer than is comfortable. We separate the
signal from the story we wanted the signal to tell.

:::callout
A method is only useful when it survives a result you did not want.
:::

:::

---scene---

:::section title="Why It Holds"

Discipline is portable. A research habit built on emotional regulation
travels across domains: finance, policy, parenting, design.

:::
