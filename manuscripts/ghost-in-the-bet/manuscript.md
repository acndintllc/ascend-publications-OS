# Chapter One — The Even-Money Lie

The casino floor at four in the morning has a particular silence, the kind that
only money knows how to make. Mira stood at the rail of the high-limit pit and
watched the chips move the way other people watched weather.

She had been told her whole life that gambling was a feeling. It was not. It was
a ledger pretending to be a feeling, and the house preferred you mistake one for
the other.

# Chapter Two — Tells

There is a difference between the players who lose and the players who are
losing. Mira had become exquisite at the difference. The losing ones still had
a story they were telling themselves about variance. The lost ones had stopped
telling stories at all.

That night, she would teach a man named Ari the difference, and he would not
forgive her for it.
