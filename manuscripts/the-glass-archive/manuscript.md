:::chapter-opener eyebrow="Prologue"
# The Glass Archive
:::

They built the archive out of glass so that nothing could be hidden, and
then they discovered that glass is the most opaque material of all — it
shows you exactly what the light wants you to see.

:::pullquote
Transparency, like silence, is a kind of authorship.
:::

:::section title="First Visit"

The reading room admitted me on a Tuesday. The catalogue, when I asked
for it, was handed to me in a cloth-bound volume that smelled of cedar
and apology.

:::dialogue speaker="Archivist"
You'll find the index incomplete.
:::

:::dialogue speaker="Vauclair"
Incomplete on purpose, or merely incomplete?
:::

:::dialogue speaker="Archivist"
That distinction has not been settled.
:::

:::
